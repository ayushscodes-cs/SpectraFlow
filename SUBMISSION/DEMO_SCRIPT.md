# Demonstration Video Script & Production Guide
Target Duration: 90–120 Seconds

---

## 🎬 Shot List & Narration Script

### [00:00 - 00:15] Hook: The Problem of Screen Apnea and Cognitive Burnout
- **Visual:** Close-up of developer at desk coding, followed by app title screen on desktop: "SpectraFlow: Bio-Adaptive Deep Work Studio".
- **Narration:**
  > "When we are deep in thought solving complex problems, something strange happens to our bodies: we subconsciously hold our breath—a phenomenon known as screen apnea—while suppressing our blink rate to near zero. Most focus apps are dumb clocks that don't care how your body feels. Meet SpectraFlow."

### [00:15 - 00:35] Camera Consent & Local Privacy Gate
- **Visual:** Show the Consent Gate popping up upon launch. Point cursor at the 100% Local Optical Processing guarantee. Click "Grant Consent & Enter Studio".
- **Narration:**
  > "Privacy is the core foundation of SpectraFlow. Before any sensor starts, an explicit Consent Gate explains exactly what signals are processed. No video frames ever leave local volatile memory, and nothing is saved to disk."

### [00:35 - 01:10] Live SmartSpectra Sensing & Dynamic Bio-Adaptation
- **Visual:** Click "Start Bio-Adaptive Sprint". The hardware webcam status turns active. Live optical PPG (rose line) and respiratory tidal wave (teal line) begin rendering on the canvas in real time. Show the Flow Coherence score reacting.
- **Narration:**
  > "Powered by the Presage SmartSpectra SDK, SpectraFlow extracts optical pulse rate, breathing waveforms, and blink cadence through a standard webcam. Watch the live plethysmograph and respiratory traces update synchronously at 30 frames per second."

### [01:10 - 01:30] Screen Apnea Alert & Generative Bio-Audio
- **Visual:** Narrator deliberately pauses breath for 7 seconds. The screen displays the gentle rose banner: "Screen Apnea Detected: Respiratory motion paused. Take a gentle diaphragmatic inhale." Turn on the Bio-Audio toggle. Show how the audio filter cutoff opens up as breathing resumes.
- **Narration:**
  > "If I freeze my breath during an intense coding bug, SpectraFlow catches it instantly, offering a gentle cue to restore oxygen flow. Meanwhile, our built-in generative soundscape tunes its ambient harmonics in real time to match my breathing rhythm."

### [01:30 - 01:50] Session Conclusion & Telemetry Ledger
- **Visual:** Click "Stop Sensing & Complete Sprint". The hardware releases immediately. The Sprint Telemetry Ledger modal pops up with total duration, peak flow coherence, and apnea interruptions.
- **Narration:**
  > "When the sprint ends, the camera is released instantly, giving you an objective telemetry ledger of your cognitive endurance. That is SpectraFlow—bringing genuine human physiological sensing into every knowledge worker's daily workflow."
